# Brand Guidelines Specification

## Project: Website Potensi Desa Karamatwangi
### Status: Approved
### Version: 1.0.0
### Date: 2026-07-10

---

## 1. Brand Strategic Foundation

### 1.1. Brand Vision
To position Desa Karamatwangi as a leading model of a digitally empowered, economically vibrant, and culturally rich rural community in Indonesia, accessible and inviting to the global audience.

### 1.2. Brand Mission
To showcase the authentic potential of Desa Karamatwangi by bridging the gap between local resources (entrepreneurs, natural beauty, agriculture) and external visitors through a premium, modern, and interactive digital storefront.

### 1.3. Brand Values
- **Authenticity:** We showcase real people, real products, and real landscapes. No stock corporate filters.
- **Warmth:** We represent the welcoming spirit and community bond of the village.
- **Empowerment:** We exist to lift local merchants (UMKM) and showcase their trade directly.
- **Sustainability:** Our structure is built to grow responsibly, modularly, and dynamically.

### 1.4. Brand Personality
If Desa Karamatwangi were a person, they would be a warm, knowledgeable local guide. They are deeply rooted in their heritage, proud of their natural surroundings, yet modern, forward-thinking, and digitally articulate.

### 1.5. Brand Positioning
The platform sits between traditional municipal information boards and premium travel directories. It is **not** a bureaucratic portal; it is an immersive, map-centric destination explorer and local catalog.

### 1.6. Brand Promise
"Every interaction on this platform connects you directly to the life, trade, and beauty of Karamatwangi."

## 2. Visual Identity & Logo Philosophy

### 2.1. Logo Philosophy
The visual brand mark representing Desa Karamatwangi combines three key conceptual elements in a clean geometric shape:
1. **The Mountains (Mount Cikuray context):** Symbolizing the natural geography, fresh climate, and grandeur of the landscape.
2. **The Leaf / Crop:** Representing agricultural prosperity, sustainability, and fertile land.
3. **The Interlocking Nodes:** Representing community connection, digital map markers, and cooperation (gotong royong).

### 2.2. Logo Usage
- **Primary Logo:** Logomark + "Potensi Desa Karamatwangi" in Plus Jakarta Sans. To be rendered on light neutral backgrounds.
- **Monochrome Logo:** Used for high-contrast constraints or pure white header overlays.
- **Icon Mark:** Used as the map pin anchor, browser favicon, and CMS dashboard avatar.

### 2.3. Clear Space
To preserve visual breathing room and Apple-inspired minimalism, keep a clear space surrounding the logo equal to at least 50% of the logo mark's height on all sides.

### 2.4. Minimum Size
- **Screen rendering:** Minimum height of `32px` to guarantee legibility on standard mobile phone viewports.
- **Icon mark only:** Minimum height of `16px`.

### 2.5. Incorrect Usage
- ❌ Do not apply drop shadows to the logo mark.
- ❌ Do not stretch, skew, or rotate the typography.
- ❌ Do not place the primary colored logo over busy background images without a dark overlay gradient.
- ❌ Do not combine the logo mark with standard bureaucratic government emblems on the same visual header.

## 3. Color & Typography Personalities

### 3.1. Color Personality
Each color in our palette communicates an emotional state derived directly from the landscape of Karamatwangi:
- **Forest Green (`#16A34A`):** The primary color represents fertility, natural growth, the surrounding tea/agricultural plantations, and environmental sustainability. It evokes *calmness, freshness, and trust*.
- **Warm Amber (`#D97706`):** The secondary color represents the warmth of the community, local craftsmanship (bamboo, wood), and harvest prosperity. It evokes *optimism, creativity, and energy*.
- **Sky Blue (`#0EA5E9`):** The accent color represents clean water springs, clear skies, and open horizons. It evokes *clarity, modern tech integration, and spaciousness*.
- **Stone Neutrals (`#FAFAF9` to `#1C1917`):** The warm stone scale replaces raw administrative grays. It feels *grounded, premium, and organic*, mirroring the village's soil and mountain rocks.

### 3.2. Typography Personality
- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Headings):** Selected for its geometric structure, clean spacing, and modern editorial feel. It removes the standard rigid, academic layout typical of government portals, replacing it with a premium, destination-first experience.
- **Inter (Body):** Selected for its exceptional legibility on mobile viewports. It is neutral, comfortable, and ensures that descriptions of local products can be consumed without eye strain.

## 4. Asset Guidelines (Photography & Illustrations)

### 4.1. Photography Style
All photography must strictly adhere to the following parameters:
- **Real Over Stock:** Show actual villagers, true agricultural fields, and authentic products. Generic corporate images (e.g. handshake in a high-rise office) are strictly banned.
- **Natural Lighting:** Capture scenes in natural daylight, emphasizing the morning mist or golden afternoon glow typical of Karamatwangi's mountainous geography.
- **Focus Areas:**
  - *Landscapes:* Lush mountain vistas, crop terraces, and natural springs.
  - *UMKM:* Close-ups of hands crafting, food preparation, and local merchants smiling.
  - *Agriculture:* Freshly harvested vegetables, coffee beans, and green fields.

### 4.2. Illustration Style
Illustrations should be used sparingly as empty state indicators or onboarding helpers:
- **Style:** Flat, minimal vector art with thin outline borders (matching Lucide icon stroke weight).
- **Colors:** Restrict fills to the primary earth colors (greens, ambers, warm stone backgrounds).

### 4.3. Icon Style
- **Library:** Lucide Icons.
- **Stroke Weight:** `1.5px` (thin, minimal, premium).
- **Consistency:** Always pair icons with text labels or explicit ARIA tags to ensure screen readers do not bypass navigation.

## 5. Brand Voice & Writing Rules

### 5.1. Writing Style
The platform communicates as an inviting host. We write with clarity, simplicity, and active phrasing, omitting rigid, passive government terminology:

| Traditional Government Portal Style | Digital Village Showcase Style |
| --- | --- |
| *"Daftar Data Inventarisasi UMKM Desa"* | **"Jelajahi Produk Lokal"** (Explore Local Products) |
| *"Statistik Demografi Kependudukan"* | **"Village Insights"** (Village Insights) |
| *"Hubungi Admin Pengaduan Masyarakat"* | **"Hubungi Kami"** (Get in Touch) |
| *"User login untuk melakukan administrasi"* | **"Masuk ke Dashboard"** (Enter Dashboard) |

### 5.2. UI Tone Examples
- **Primary CTA:** Action-focused. Instead of *"Buka Peta"*, use **"Mulai Menjelajahi Peta"** (Start Exploring the Map).
- **Error Messages:** Friendly and helpful. Instead of *"Error 500: Database connection timeout"*, use **"Koneksi terputus sejenak. Ayo coba muat kembali halaman ini."** (Connection lost for a moment. Let's try reloading this page).
- **Notifications:** Direct and clean. Instead of *"Data berhasil dimasukkan ke record database"*, use **"Profil UMKM berhasil diterbitkan!"** (UMKM profile successfully published!).

## 6. SEO Naming Guidelines
Page titles must maintain a clean, readable structure optimized for both search crawlers and human visitors:
- **Homepage:** `Karamatwangi — Jelajahi Potensi & Produk Lokal Desa`
- **Map Page:** `Peta Interaktif Potensi Desa Karamatwangi`
- **Potentials Directory:** `Daftar Potensi & UMKM Desa Karamatwangi`
- **Detail Pages:** `[Nama UMKM / Potensi] — Potensi Desa Karamatwangi`

---

## 7. Future ACA Integration Branding
As new categories (Tourism, Agriculture, Livestock, etc.) are introduced via the Adaptive Content Architecture (ACA), they inherit this brand system automatically:
- **Wisconsin Green/Forest Green** remains the primary visual anchor for maps and navigation.
- Category sub-pages copy the warm, editorial storytelling layout.
- The voice remains helpful, active, and focused on showcasing local pride rather than administrative data collection.
