# COPYWRITING_GUIDE.md

# Tone of Voice

Friendly.

Simple.

Natural.

Optimistic.

Human.

---

Avoid

Government language.

Complicated sentences.

Marketing buzzwords.

Over-promising.

---

Preferred Writing Style

Short paragraphs.

Clear headings.

Action-oriented CTAs.

Storytelling.

---

CTA Examples

Explore Village

Discover Tourism

Meet Local UMKM

Read Stories

Visit Karamatwangi

Plan Your Journey

---

Writing Rule

One section.

One message.

One action.

---

Homepage Goal

Curiosity.

Not information overload.

---

Tourism Goal

Make visitors want to visit.

---

UMKM Goal

Build trust.

---

History Goal

Create emotional connection.

---

Contact Goal

Make contacting easy.
