# Project Vision

Portal Potensi Desa Karamatwangi

Version 1.0

---

## Our Purpose

This is not just a website.

This is the digital identity of Desa Karamatwangi.

The website should introduce the village through meaningful stories, authentic visuals, and a modern digital experience.

Every visitor should leave with one thought:

"I want to visit this place."

---

## Our Mission

Reveal the hidden beauty of Desa Karamatwangi.

Promote local tourism.

Support local UMKM.

Celebrate culture.

Empower the community.

Create a digital experience that represents the village with pride.

---

## Emotional Goal

Visitors should feel:

Curious.

Calm.

Inspired.

Connected.

Welcome.

The website should create emotion before providing information.

---

## First Impression

Within the first five seconds, visitors should understand that:

Desa Karamatwangi is surrounded by nature.

The village has unique tourism potential.

The community is active.

The village is modern but still rooted in its traditions.

---

## Experience

The homepage is a journey.

Not a dashboard.

Not a government portal.

Every scroll should feel like discovering another part of the village.

Arrival

↓

Introduction

↓

Nature

↓

Culture

↓

People

↓

Products

↓

Invitation

---

## Visual Identity

Nature comes first.

Technology supports the experience.

Minimalism creates clarity.

Photography tells the story.

Whitespace creates elegance.

Motion guides attention.

---

## Mobile Experience

Most visitors will use mobile devices.

Therefore:

Mobile is the primary platform.

Desktop is an enhanced experience.

Every interaction should feel comfortable with one thumb.

Navigation should always be effortless.

Content should never feel overwhelming.

---

## Storytelling

Every section should answer a simple question.

Who are we?

↓

Why is this village special?

↓

What can visitors experience?

↓

What products are made here?

↓

How can people visit or connect?

Users should never feel like they are reading documentation.

They should feel like they are exploring.

---

## Photography

Real photos are one of the most important assets.

Whenever possible, use photography from Desa Karamatwangi itself.

Morning mist.

Forest.

Mountains.

Rice fields.

Local people.

Traditional activities.

UMKM.

Community events.

Avoid generic stock photography.

Authenticity always wins.

---

## Motion Philosophy

Motion should feel natural.

Like wind moving leaves.

Like clouds crossing mountains.

Like morning mist disappearing.

Motion should never exist just to look impressive.

Every animation should have purpose.

---

## Performance Philosophy

Fast experiences build trust.

Beautiful experiences keep attention.

The website should achieve both.

Never sacrifice usability for visual effects.

---

## Long-Term Vision

This website should continue to grow.

Future possibilities include:

Interactive tourism map.

Village events calendar.

UMKM marketplace.

Digital village services.

Community stories.

Village statistics.

Multi-language support.

The design should be flexible enough to support future growth without losing its identity.

---

## Success Criteria

The project is successful when:

Visitors remember the village.

Local people feel proud.

Tourists become interested.

Information is easy to access.

The experience feels premium.

Technology becomes invisible.

Nature becomes unforgettable.

---

## Design Principle

Whenever a design decision is unclear, ask:

Does this make the village feel more alive?

If the answer is yes,

it is probably the right decision.
