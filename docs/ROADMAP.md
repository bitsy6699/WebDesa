# Vision

Create a premium mobile-first digital experience that introduces Desa Karamatwangi through storytelling, authentic visuals, and modern interaction.

---

# Success Criteria

The website should:

- Feel modern
- Feel natural
- Feel premium
- Be mobile-first
- Load fast
- Be accessible
- Represent Desa Karamatwangi authentically

# Development Workflow

For every sprint:

↓

Design Decision

↓

Implementation

↓

Review

↓

Refinement

↓

Complete

Never sacrifice simplicity.

Technology is the tool.

Mobile is the priority.

Storytelling is the experience.

Every sprint should improve the project.

Never redesign for the sake of redesign.
